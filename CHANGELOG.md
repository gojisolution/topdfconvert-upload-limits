# Changelog

Every addition and re-check, newest first. Dates are when we read the authority's own page.

## 2026-11-22

- Repository renamed from `official-upload-limits` to `topdfconvert-upload-limits` (the old address redirects here). No rule changed.

## 2026-11-21

- Added **USCIS online filing**: 12 MB per file; PDF, JPG or JPEG (TIFF for some forms); no encrypted or password-protected files. Source: uscis.gov, page reviewed/updated 10/07/2026.
- Added **Meu INSS** (Brazil): 5 MB per document; PDF, scanned in colour, whole document in frame. Source: gov.br/inss, updated 28/10/2026.
- Re-checked **IRCC**: the page (modified 2026-11-03) now lists six account types. 4 MB for the IRCC secure account, online citizenship application, asylum portal and Permanent Residence Portal; 5 MB for the IRCC Portal and its new version.

## 2026-11-20

- First version: **NVC / CEAC** (2 MB, 150 DPI, one document per file), **IRCC** (4 MB and 5 MB) and the **Indian e-Visa** (documents 10–300 KB).
